# AOS mood reference: Nous Research

Status: accepted mood evidence, not a design specification.

The Hermes Agent and Nous Portal sites are the closest mood reference identified so far. Their layouts and identity should not be copied or treated as a base to modify.

## What carries the mood

The strongest material is the illustrated world: engraving-like figures, blank faces, hands, impossible bodies, radiating lines, halftone textures, damaged computer images, and fragments of old interfaces. The figures feel intelligent without feeling human. That tension suits a system made from many specialised machine agents.

The extreme light-dark and colour contrast makes the product feel decisive. It commits across the entire surface instead of decorating isolated controls.

The best compositions combine a disciplined frame with one unruly image. The structure stays legible while the imagery creates pressure and scale. The cloud feature grid is a weaker reference where irregularity appears applied rather than caused by the content.

The small hand-and-orb emblem works because it is simple, symbolic, and slightly unsettling. AOS needs an equally memorable symbol derived from its own mechanism.

## Translation into AOS

Use the agent swarm as the source of visual tension. Calm controls surround a living field of branching work, compressed branches, conflicting hypotheses, dormant agents, and bursts of activity. Density and distortion should correspond to actual conditions such as expansion, uncertainty, contention, or failure.

Develop an original image language around distributed intelligence. Promising material includes faceless scientific specimens, impossible instruments, branching anatomical plates, calibration marks, and machine-generated research diagrams. Mythological deities, the Hermes electric blue, the Nous hand-and-orb mark, and their page compositions are excluded as direct sources.

The engraved figure should dominate the emotional identity. Digital corruption and chaotic layout remain secondary materials, used where the system has something chaotic to communicate.

## Still undecided
